**Branch C.** (i) The 84 unresolved settlements contribute exactly zero to the MEASURED series and
are bracketed only in the DECISION quantity — "never treated as zero" holds for the decision input,
not the measured series. (ii) Sealing asserted by ordering only, no external anchor (UNVERIFIED).

(00363c3a, drifted by an unrelated branch's additive contract), so the freeze no longer verifies in
place — inert for the result because the gross reproduces independently from the panel and raw
archives, but recorded. (iv) V3 established the kill is SIGN-ROBUST: reversing the structure gives

  transferable across branches; (3) no universalised ratio bars (the Nasdaq 5×/10× bars stay local);

  corrections do **not** do: admit data, verify a cost, pass a gate, or authorize capital.
- **D-0047** — procedural memory, the six durable lessons: (1) archive/grid joins must verify bucket
  IDENTITY against a bounds-derived grid, never counts; (2) transport markers do not prove session
  completeness (a 0.92% head+tail splice passes gzip CRC/ISIZE, byte counts, framing and terminal
  markers) — predeclare a continuity/coverage certificate over the measurement window; (3)
  specification-transcribed directory/enumeration fields must be verified against that
  specification's own appendix before they gate a universe (one sub-type character selected 31 of
  12,809 symbols); (4) a frozen contract's declared code hashes must be re-verified in place at the
  end of a campaign (an unrelated later edit to a shared module broke a freeze's verifiability); (5)
  pre-outcome sealing needs an external anchor — mtimes do not resist a re-seal, so future freezes
  should be committed or RFC3161-timestamped **before** the measurement runs; (6) long-running
  workers must checkpoint their summary artifact before the end of the job (one producer died at its
  reporting step after completing its run).
