## Question being answered

What is E(markout | fill, state), and does adverse selection reverse the apparent edge?

## Why public evidence cannot answer it

Conditional markouts require the project's own fills under its own order placement; no public analogue exists for these tuples.

## Dataset required

Simulated fills from PASSIVE_FILL_MODEL, later reconciled against shadow and micro-live fills.

## Primary metric

Median and tail markout conditional on fill, in bps after costs

## Null hypothesis

Exposure-matched random fills at the same timestamps.

## Baselines

Unconditional markout over the same window

## Kill criterion

Conditional markout is negative after costs at every queue position.
