# 07 — Monitoring and Source Policy

## Objective

Detect strictly new Jev/System-One developments quickly without turning social-media hype into technical truth.

## Discovery order

1. Public X/Twitter posts from builders/researchers — fast lead discovery.
2. Linked GitHub commits/releases/issues.
3. Hugging Face model/dataset cards and weights.
4. arXiv / conference papers.
5. TypeSafe docs/changelog/evals.
6. Independent reproductions and benchmarks.
7. Reddit/Discord/community discussion as lead generation.

## Alert requirements

An alert must answer:
- What is new since the previous observation?
- When was it posted/released?
- Is it a claim, code release, dataset, benchmark, reproduction, or negative result?
- Is the evidence self-reported or independently reproduced?
- Does it strengthen, weaken, or leave unchanged our trading-runtime thesis?
- What experiment should change because of it?

Do not re-alert unchanged material.

## Important limitation

Public web search does not guarantee complete, immediate indexing of every X post, especially login-gated or poorly indexed content. Therefore X is treated as a high-speed lead source, not a complete event stream. Claims should be confirmed through linked primary artifacts wherever possible.
