**Scope:** current canonical repository; `.omp/` inspected only; no dead candidate resurrected; no live or paper trading; no data purchased.

## Decision summary

No surviving tradable candidate earns `TEST_NOW`. Every candidate with meaningful expected research value requires at least one external action before a valid causal, after-friction experiment can run: operator access/jurisdiction facts, broker/FCM fee terms, exchange/vendor historical-data access, or a historical sample whose timestamp semantics can be validated. The repository currently contains no owned/free replay dataset for any surviving branch that is sufficient for its stated causal experiment.

The Nasdaq large-tick H1–H3 top-of-book sequence remains closed. `DEAD_ENDS.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.md`, and decisions D-0037/D-0038 contain no new resurrection evidence. The three Nasdaq large-tick rows are therefore not reconsidered.

This is not `M1_FAILED_OR_PARTIAL`: the evidence does not establish that every surviving mechanism is economically impossible. It establishes that autonomous research cannot presently run the cheapest valid falsifier without an external prerequisite.

## Comparative conclusion

The highest decision value per unit autonomous cost is not another public literature pass. The strongest conditional frontier is the **CME ES H3 OFI aggressive row** because it has a named liquid instrument, a bounded 1–15 s causal question, a direct technical observation path, and a cheap gross-magnitude falsifier that can precede execution modelling. It is nevertheless `EXTERNAL_BLOCK`, not `TEST_NOW`: the required historical sample/timestamp validation and exact account-level cost path are not owned, and the project forbids purchasing data or inventing costs. ES H1 passive, BZX passive and cross-venue stale quotes carry additional queue/synchronization burden. Crypto spot has very high verified fee floors but lacks a sourced gross bound and operator facts. Hyperliquid H4 is the most plausible alternative only after access and dated history are supplied.

## Conditional minimum experiment (not authorized by this goal)

This is recorded to make the external block actionable; it does **not** promote the candidate to `TEST_NOW`.

- **Candidate:** `TUP-CME-ES-H3-OFI-AGG`
- **Mechanism:** signed order-flow imbalance, defined before outcome as aggressive signed volume relative to contemporaneous executable depth/trade activity on one exact ES contract.
- **Decision question:** Does the causal H3 signal have gross displacement of the same order as the exact all-in round-trip friction, before any model, queue simulator or live path is built?
- **Data:** one historical CME event/trade sample with sequence numbers, exchange timestamps, contract/security definitions and validated clock semantics; one exact ES contract and preregistered roll rule. Status: external sample/quote/access required; not owned/free in the current repository.
- **Causal state:** information available at decision timestamp only; no future messages, no ex-post selection, no ML oracle.
- **Outcome:** side-signed mid/executable-price markout at fixed preregistered H3 delays, with coverage and confidence intervals.
- **Friction comparator:** exact account-level exchange, clearing, FCM/commission, spread and declared execution-cost floor; do not substitute generic CME or industry values.
- **Null:** no conditional displacement beyond the unconditional signed markout / no economically material difference from the predeclared null state.
- **Primary metric:** pooled gross markout divided by the exact round-trip cost floor, with the cost ledger and horizon fixed before reading results.
- **Kill criterion:** a predeclared pooled ratio below the project's admission bar, or a non-positive gross markout after the declared cost comparator; the numerical bar must be fixed in the successor experiment specification before data inspection.
- **Continuation criterion:** positive, causally valid gross headroom over the declared friction comparator with adequate coverage and no timestamp or leakage failure; this only authorizes true M2 work.
- **Prohibited post-result tuning:** no changing contract, horizon, OFI bins, universe, roll rule, cost tier, side convention, threshold, or state selection after observing outcomes; no oracle or ML feature.
- **Stopping condition:** stop after the fixed sample, fixed state space and fixed horizons are evaluated, or immediately on failed timestamp/identity validation. Do not proceed to full M2 strategy evaluation in reselection.
